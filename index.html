Independent Marine and Cargo Surveyor

PT MareNova International Maritime Surveyors is a professional company engaged in marine surveying and inspection services, operating on both national and international scales. Founded on a strong commitment to integrity, accuracy, and professionalism, MareNova is dedicated to providing reliable solutions for the maritime, petroleum, and global logistics industries. Supported by experienced and internationally certified surveyors, MareNova offers a wide range of services including Petroleum & Gas Survey, Marine Survey, P&I Service, Vegetable Oil & Chemical Survey, and Container Inspection. Every inspection is conducted in compliance with international safety and quality standards to ensure precise, transparent, and dependable results.
